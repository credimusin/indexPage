[Project: CashFlow360]
Title:      Cashflow360
Repository: https://github.com/credimusin/CashFlow360
Platform:   Web (Anti-Spreadsheet Defense Grid)

Description:
A lightweight, sanity-preserving budgeting and resource-tracking engine
designed to rescue humans from the psychological trauma of 400-megabyte
corporate Excel files.

History & Evolution:
- Phase 1 (The Great Spreadsheet Rebellion):
  Born in the dark trenches of enterprise IT, where critical budget
  numbers were stored inside a cursed 'FINAL_v2_REAL_USE_THIS.xlsx'
  file that crashed whenever two people looked at it at the same time.
  BMO built the first prototype to prove that entering numbers into a
  form shouldn't require a sacrifice to the Macro Gods.

- Phase 2 (The Bureaucracy Boss Fight):
  Evolved into a full departmental ledger system. Unfortunately, middle
  management discovered that transparent automated tracking left zero
  room for "mysterious coffee & snacks rounding errors," leading to an
  epic stalemate between clean software architecture and corporate
  inertia.

- Phase 3 (The Immortal PWA Edition):
  Reforged from scratch as a sleek, self-contained Progressive Web App
  capable of running on a potato-sized server (primarily used today by
  BMO to audit galactic chocolate chip cookie reserves and AA battery
  depreciation).

Technical Features:
- Functional Paradigm: Written with direct, predictable state logic
  rather than 14 layers of abstract EnterpriseSingletonFactoryBean OOP.
- Zero Container Bloat: Engineered to run lean on bare metal without
  needing a Kubernetes cluster just to add two integers together.
- Vanilla JS Frontend: Instant load times, offline-capable PWA shell,
  and a clean transaction approval workflow.

Usage:
Fork the repository, deploy it on a toaster, and delete Excel forever.

Tech stack: Python, Django, SQLite, PostgreSQL, Vanilla JS, PWA.
