[Project: Inner Light]
Title:    Inner Light
Status:   Standalone Offline Artifact
Platform: Android (APK)

Description:
A quiet, 100% offline mobile sanctuary for reading classical philosophy,
ancient wisdom, and contemplative texts - built with zero algorithms,
zero cloud telemetry, and zero push notifications.

History & Evolution:
Modern mobile apps are designed like slot machines: infinite scroll,
blinking red badges, "Suggested For You" slop, and 47 background
trackers fighting over your battery life. Even meditation apps now
demand a monthly cloud subscription just to play 10 minutes of rain
sounds.

Inner Light was engineered as the exact opposite: an air-gapped pocket
library containing centuries of human wisdom (from Marcus Aurelius and
Lao Tzu to sacred scriptures across world traditions) plus a strictly
local encrypted-by-isolation SQLite journal that never phones home.

Behind-the-scenes hardware lore: While compiling the release Android APK
with the heavy Gradle toolchain, the build machine hit severe thermal
throttling and kept black-screening mid-compile. Refusing to surrender,
the operator physically held the laptop directly inside the icy blast of
an air conditioner like a supersonic wind tunnel until the APK build
crossed the finish line at 100%!

Technical Features:
- Multi-Tradition Offline Library: Complete classical and philosophical
  corpora stored locally for instant zero-latency reading in airplane
  mode or deep space.
- Customizable Daily Practices: Organize personal reading lists,
  stoic reflections, and morning/evening routines.
- Strictly Local Journal: Stored exclusively on-device in SQLite - zero
  accounts, zero cloud sync, zero analytics SDKs.

Usage:
Install the standalone APK, enable Airplane Mode, and touch grass.

Tech stack: React Native, Expo, SQLite (expo-sqlite), Tailwind CSS
(NativeWind), Android SDK.
