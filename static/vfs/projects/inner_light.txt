[Project: Inner Light]
Title:    Inner Light
Website:  https://innerlight.imaginal.dev/
Platform: Android (APK)

Description:
A quiet, distraction-free offline mobile reader for sacred texts,
world philosophy, and personal contemplation, complete with a
customizable prayer book and a local spiritual journal.

History & Evolution:
It all began right after my 39th birthday - on May 13th, to be exact
(time really flies, O_O). I never considered myself a traditionally
religious person, but world religions and classical philosophy have
always captivated me as profound cultural phenomena and mirrors for
exploring one's own inner world. At a certain point in life, you
naturally find yourself slowing down and looking inward for balance,
clarity, and peace. I didn't want any algorithms, social feeds, ads,
cloud logins, or push notifications - I just wanted a quiet, intimate
sanctuary on my phone. A portable place to study sacred scriptures,
read inspiring wisdom across traditions, and keep private notes without
anyone watching.

The earliest prototype was born under the name "Hymns". It started as
a humble personal collection of inspiring quotes, prayers, and
affirmations. But as development progressed, I realized that texts from
different world traditions - Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism,
and Stoic philosophy - deserve deep reverence and careful separation,
rather than being jumbled together. That led to the core design of
Inner Light: an onboarding step where everyone chooses their own
tradition and personal path, whether through a single doctrine,
comparative reflection, or quiet daily meditation.

Bringing the mobile build to life was an adventure in itself. A funny
behind-the-scenes memory: while compiling the release APK with the
Android build toolchain, my aging laptop hit severe thermal throttling,
overheated, and kept crashing mid-build. Determined to finish the
release, I literally stood holding the laptop directly under the icy
airflow of my room's air conditioner, cooling the chassis like a wind
tunnel until the APK compilation finally succeeded! :D

Technical Features:
- Comprehensive Scripture Library: Full Old and New Testaments in the
  Russian Synodal translation, the Holy Quran and 40 Hadiths, Buddhist
  sutras (Dhammapada, Heart Sutra, Diamond Sutra), Hindu scriptures
  (Bhagavad Gita, Yoga Sutras, Upanishads), and Stoic/spiritual
  philosophy (Marcus Aurelius, Lao Tzu, Kahlil Gibran).
- Customizable Prayer Book & Practices: Daily morning and evening
  prayers, duas, mantras, and affirmations, with the ability to add,
  edit, and organize your own custom texts.
- Private Local Journal: A built-in reflective diary stored strictly
  on-device in a local SQLite database - zero cloud sync, zero
  telemetry, and zero tracking.
- 100% Offline & Independent: Everything runs completely offline with
  no internet access required, distributed directly as standalone
  APK releases signed with my personal GPG key.

Usage:
Download the standalone APK from the website, install it on your
Android phone, and carry a quiet library of wisdom in your pocket.

Tech stack: React Native, Expo, SQLite (expo-sqlite), Tailwind CSS
(NativeWind v4), Android SDK.
