=== ABOUT THIS PROJECT ===

Welcome, traveler! You have somehow stumbled into BMO's pocket dimension.

Let's be honest: 99% of normal humans who land on this page stare at the
animated sky for four seconds, think "Wait... what is this and why is there
no 'Buy Now' button?", and slowly back away.

If you are part of the remaining 1% who immediately pressed the backtick
key (`), opened the console, and started snooping through the directory tree
- congratulations, you are home.

[ THE VIBE & SPIRIT ]
This entire space is an interactive retro-futuristic sandbox and a monument
to joyful over-engineering. It has no corporate sales funnel, no newsletter
popups, and no serious business purpose other than existing as a digital
playground full of synthetic beeps, questionable advice, hidden ciphers,
and terminal Easter eggs.

[ CRAFTSMANSHIP & POTATO ENGINEERING ]
While the resident OS doesn't take itself seriously at all, the machinery
under the hood is handcrafted with care: zero bloated frontend frameworks,
zero megabyte-heavy dependency black holes, and 100% free-range vanilla
JavaScript, CSS variables, and HTML5 Canvas running on two virtual AA
batteries and a warm chocolate chip cookie.
