=== ABOUT THIS PROJECT ===

Welcome, traveler! You have arrived in my personal corner of cyberspace.

This website is an interactive retro-futuristic space and developer
portfolio — a place where I showcase my engineering work, share a glimpse
into who I am, and provide direct channels to get in touch.

[ THE VIBE & SPIRIT ]
The entire environment is crafted with a playful, nostalgic terminal
charm, seasoned with tongue-in-cheek humor, interactive quirks, and hidden
Easter eggs. If you're the kind of curious mind who enjoys looking under
the hood, poking at configuration files, and testing unexpected commands,
there is plenty here for you to uncover.

[ CRAFTSMANSHIP & PASSION ]
While the interface doesn't take itself too seriously, the engineering
behind it is genuine. Built from the ground up without heavy frameworks,
this project is a live demonstration of my technical depth, dedication, and
attention to detail. It was designed with a clear understanding of the stack
and a relentless passion for technology and continuous growth — showing how
I approach software: clean, thoughtful, resilient, and crafted with care.

[ SYSTEM NAVIGATION ]
